Botanic Bandit, AKA Earth Burglar

Founded by Alex Neiwert, Earth Burglar is a maker of worlds and stories. She helps organize thoughts and turns them into realities. There are ways to measure what is around us and in our heads. She sticks to these places for getting the word out: Instagram, YouTube, Itch.io, and ArtStation.

The Earthburglar has been around since One can remember. The things that are tucked away, squirrled away for the day when you will understand what it was all for.
